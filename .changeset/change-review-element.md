Review screen for a subscription change. The new element `<pricing-change-review>` (class `PricingChangeReviewElement`) renders a `SelectionComparison` in the light DOM: the plan with its relation, the price before and after, the add-ons, features and usage limits that change, a summary of what stays the same, the notices and the error of the host, and the Confirm and Back buttons. It emits the cancelable events `pricing-change-confirm` and `pricing-change-back`. It is registered by the new subpath `pricing-renderer/define-change-review` (or by `definePricingChangeReview()`) and rendered in React by `PricingChangeReview`, which registers the tag when it renders; `pricing-renderer/define` and the React entry still register only `pricing-renderer`. The text is localized with the `pricing.review*` keys (`en-US` and `es-ES`), and the styles, the `data-pr-part` hooks, the dark theme, forced colors and reduced motion are in `base.css`. Every name, notice and error is rendered as text, never as HTML.
