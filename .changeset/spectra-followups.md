The React adapter now accepts the new `onlyDifferences`, `toolbarThreshold` and `addOnFilterThreshold` props. Tag and badge labels with a very long unbroken word now wrap inside the renderer on narrow screens, and the docs list every rendered part hook, including the seven that were missing: `comparison-empty`, `addon-toolbar`, `addon-empty`, `selection-control`, `breakdown`, `breakdown-line` and `breakdown-toggle`.
