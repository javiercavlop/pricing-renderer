New core function `compareSelections({ before, after, currentUsage?, expression? })`, exported from `pricing-renderer` with its types (`SelectionComparison`, `SelectionComparisonInput`, `SelectionRelation`, `ComparedValue` and the others listed in the API reference). Each side is a pricing and a partial selection, so a contract on one pricing version can be compared with an edit on a newer one. The result is JSON-safe and detached from the input, and lists the plan change, the added, removed (by the user or by reconciliation, with a reason) and re-quantified add-ons, the features gained, lost and changed, the usage-limit changes with `delta`, `direction` and optional `currentUsage` and `exceedsCurrentUsage`, the price of both sides with `difference` (left out when a quote is needed or the currencies differ), the version change with the ids that exist in only one version, and the reconciled selection of each side. `relation` is `'current'`, `'upgrade'`, `'downgrade'` or `'change'`; an upgrade or downgrade follows the declaration order of the plans in the after pricing, not the price. Unknown plans, billing periods, add-ons and usage-limit ids never throw: they are reported as diagnostics `PR_COMPARE_UNKNOWN_PLAN`, `PR_COMPARE_UNKNOWN_BILLING_PERIOD`, `PR_COMPARE_UNKNOWN_ADDON`, `PR_COMPARE_ADDON_DROPPED`, `PR_COMPARE_UNKNOWN_USAGE_LIMIT` and `PR_COMPARE_INVALID_USAGE`. The function needs no DOM and registers no custom element. Nothing that exists today changes.
